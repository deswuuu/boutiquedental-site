What We Offer

Care for every part
of your smile.

Book a Consultation
Oral health screening, cleaning, new patient exams, X-rays.
Routine cleanings, comprehensive exams, and digital X-rays are the foundation of long-term oral health. Regular checkups help us catch issues early, when they are simpler to treat.
Same-day appointments for pain or dental emergencies.
We reserve time in our daily schedule for dental emergencies. If you have sudden pain, a cracked tooth, or a lost filling, call us and we will fit you in the same day whenever possible.
Deep scaling, gum disease treatment, night guards.
Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy mouth. We diagnose and treat gum disease through deep scaling and ongoing periodontal care, and we offer custom night guards for patients who grind or clench.
Teeth whitening, Invisalign.
Professional whitening and Invisalign clear aligners to refine the look of your smile. Whitening treatments are tailored to the shade and sensitivity of your teeth, and Invisalign offers a discreet alternative to traditional braces for both teens and adults.
Fillings and bondings.
Tooth-coloured fillings and bondings restore the function and appearance of teeth affected by decay, chips, or wear. We use modern composite materials chosen to match your natural tooth shade so repairs blend in seamlessly.
Crowns, bridges, veneers, extractions.
We handle most major restorative work in-house, including planning and fitting, so patients can complete their treatment without being referred to an outside specialist.

Why Us?

Your oral health
isn't one-size-fits-all.

The Boutique Condo Dental was founded in 2018 to bring a different kind of dental experience to downtown Toronto. We are a small practice by design, focused on getting to know our patients and tailoring care to their goals, comfort level, and health history.

We offer nitrous oxide sedation for patients who feel anxious about dental work. Our clinic uses IQAir HyperHEPA filtration, which traps 99.5% of fine and ultrafine particulates from the air, and we use digital X-rays that produce high-resolution images with significantly less radiation than traditional film.
Our team provides services in English, French, Cantonese, and Vietnamese, so you can discuss your care in the language you are most comfortable speaking.
We offer same-day appointments, online booking, and direct billing to most insurance providers (including CDCP). Our clinic is steps away from the TTC at Osgoode and St. Andrew stations, so you can get here easily before or after work.
Every treatment plan is built around each individual patient. We explain each step, check in throughout your visit, and strive to accommodate requests like headphones or extra breaks when you need them.
